## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Detailed explanation
FarmFly provides a common digital platform where farmers can register, list their agricultural products, mention quantities and set selling prices.
Buyers can browse available products and place orders directly with farmers. AI helps match farmers with suitable buyers based on product availability, location, quantity and demand.
The platform also supports GPS-based route planning and drone-assisted delivery to approved collection points wherever practical.
How it addresses the problem:
1.Reduces dependency on multiple intermediaries.
2.Connects farmers directly with buyers.
3.Improves price transparency.
4.Provides access to wider markets.
5.Reduces unnecessary delivery delays.
6.Helps consumers access fresh agricultural products.
Innovation and uniqueness:
1.AI-based farmer and buyer matching.
2.Drone-assisted delivery for suitable lightweight products.
3.GPS-based delivery tracking.
4.AI-based crop demand prediction.
5.Regional-language support.
6.Digital payment and order management.
